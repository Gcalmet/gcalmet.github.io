My PhD is about simulating the emergence of spatial organization in bacterial colonies, which could be an additional building block to explain the transition from unicellular to multicellular life and the emergence of complexity.

## Personal philosphy

Between optimism and pessimism lies **alpinism** : the art of hoping for the best while preparing for the worst.

I’m passionate about exploring new paths, embracing challenges, and turning curiosity into action—whether on the mountain through alpinism or in the world of ideas through scientific research. In both realms, I believe in learning by staying curious, sharing knowledge, and embracing the journey, even when the climb is steep and risky.
